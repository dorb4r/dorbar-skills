# 08_learn: results become rules

One job: after posting, read the results and fold the lessons into the right file.

## Inputs
- Working: `{run}/01_brief.md`
- Working: `{run}/07_owner.md`
- Project: `{project}/references/decisions.md`

Do NOT load: craft references unless a lesson changes one; other runs.

## Tools
- The platform's insights (MCP or the owner's numbers): reach, watch time, sends, saves, follows, DMs.

## Process
1. 7 days after posting, record the numbers in `{run}/08_results.md` next to the brief's goal.
2. Ask the owner one question at a time: what worked, what didn't, what surprised them.
3. Fold in: project-specific lessons → rewrite lines in `{project}/references/decisions.md`
   (belief format, with provenance). General craft lessons → propose an edit to the skill's
   reference (one rule at a time, a PR in the skills repo).
4. Move the run to `{project}/_archive/runs/`.

## Outputs
- `{run}/08_results.md`, updated `decisions.md`

## Check
Human check: the owner confirms the lessons before they're written.
