# 02_ideas: many ideas, one survivor

One job: generate ideas from real material, kill the weak ones, pitch the best three.

## Inputs
- Working: `{run}/01_brief.md`
- Reference: `../../references/story.md`
- Reference: `../../references/formats.md`
- Example: `../../examples/README.md` (open single examples only when a pitch names them)
- Project: `{project}/references/brand.md`

Do NOT load: shooting, music, editing references; past runs.

## Tools
- Skills: marketingskills `social` / `copywriting` for post-copy angles, only after the pick.

## Process
1. Mine real material first: the sources in `brand.md` → Material (idea bank, DM/consult phrasings,
   client stories the owner names, series in progress). New ideas go back into the idea bank. No invented stories; unknown facts are questions to the owner.
2. Write ≥ 10 one-sentence premises, each tagged with format, emotion and sender.
3. Run the kill checklist (`story.md`) on each; keep the survivors' scores.
4. Pitch the top 3: premise, the "but", the peak, the reference example it borrows a mechanism
   from, and the footage it needs (exists / to shoot). Different formats, not three variants.

## Outputs
- `{run}/02_ideas.md` (all ideas with kill results; the 3 pitches on top)

## Check
Human check: the owner picks one (or sends it back). Write the pick and why at the top.
