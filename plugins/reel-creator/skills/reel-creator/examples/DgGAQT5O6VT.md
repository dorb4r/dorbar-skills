## Mechanism
A repeated locked wide frame is the progress meter: each return shows growth (a match cut across
hours). Details in between give texture; cuts ride the beat and section changes. The finale changes
place and light: the payoff feels like a different world. Filmed by a second person, stable, cinematic.
## Steal this
- Mark a tripod spot for one wide per stage; 1–2 beats per shot; finale in a new setting, held ≥ 5 s.
