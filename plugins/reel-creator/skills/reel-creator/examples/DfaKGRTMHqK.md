## Mechanism
A direct challenge to the viewer (stakes + an ask), a face with personality for 4.5 s, then the
proof. The post caption "Did it work?" turns every viewer into a commenter. The editing is trivial.
## Steal this
- The challenge premise in the artist's voice, with N best pieces framed identically (a fixed tripod spot).
- Put the ask in the post caption as a question, not on an end card.
