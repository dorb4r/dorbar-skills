# editing.md: cutting craft and the motion pass

Used by 06_edit. Tags: [S] sourced, [O] opinion. Sound is in `sound-design.md`, music in `music.md`.

## The order of passes [O, after the research recipe]
1. **Rough cut**: story only, no music, 10–20% long. Mark the 3 money moments (e.g. the first look, the punchline, the reveal).
2. **Music map** onto the cut (`music.md`); money moments on phrase starts and the climax.
3. **Sound pass** (`sound-design.md`).
4. **Motion pass**: one speed ramp, 2–4 punch-ins on beats, at most one shake on the drop, one signature transition. Nothing else.
5. **Text pass** (`script.md` → Words on screen).
6. **Colour**: one look for every clip, natural skin, black ink truly black, light grain to glue phone clips.
7. **QA** (`review.md`).

## Cutting [S: Murch, editing craft]
- Priority for every cut: emotion > story > rhythm > eye-trace > screen plane > 3D space. Give up from the bottom.
- **Eye-trace**: the focal point at the end of shot A should start in the same third of the frame in shot B. Faces, contrast and saturated colour pull the eye.
- **Cut on action**: mid-movement (a wipe, a hand lifting, a turn). Enter the next shot with the motion already underway.
- **Match cut**: the same shape or position across time (drawing → stencil → skin; the anchor wide at each stage). Needs near-identical framing (drift < ~10%).
- **J/L cuts**: sound leads into a shot (J) or a laugh carries over the next (L), 0.2–0.4 s.
- **Shot size**: wide → medium → close → extreme close → pull out once for the payoff. No two adjacent shots of the same size and angle (30° rule); fix with a 110–130% punch-in, a cutaway, or a cut on the wipe.
- **Hold vs cut**: hold while it shows something new; cut when the viewer has got it. Process 0.6–1.5 s, a beat with text 1.5–2.5 s, the payoff ≥ 2 s (studied pro reels hold it 5–7 s).
- **Rhythm**: never more than 3 shots of the same length; accelerate into the payoff, then stop dead.

## Motion: when it helps, when it looks cheap [O, from editor guides]
| Technique | Helps | Cheap when |
|---|---|---|
| Speed ramp | fast through the boring part, slow on contact or the reveal, landing on a beat | on every clip or on talking footage |
| Punch-in 110–120% | emphasis on a word or hit; a fake second angle on one long take | every beat; > 130% on phone footage |
| Shake 0.2–0.3 s | the drop or an impact, once | constant |
| Whip / blur transition | two moves in the same direction | between static shots |
| Mask reveal | a hand or towel passing wipes stage A to stage B | visible edges, wrong direction |
| Split screen | before/after side by side ≥ 1.5 s | panels too small to read the work |
| Freeze + label | pause on the money moment, once ("3 hours later") | more than once |
| Kinetic captions | 1–3 words, one accent word | every word bouncing |
| Light leak / flash | one soft section change | over the work or a face |
| Grain / grade | one look for all clips | heavy enough to hide line work |

## Visual systems that earn fast cutting (from `examples/`)
- **Anchor frame**: a locked wide repeated at every stage, details in between (`examples/DgGAQT5O6VT.md`).
- **Swap**: the same hand-held frame alternating blank and finished, one per beat (`examples/DgFIGapO6U5.md`).
- **Flip**: an accelerating flipbook through a stack, 0.1 s per frame at the end (`examples/DcKkXqrs_vJ.md`).
Without a system, fast cuts read as a slideshow.

## Retention [S + O]
Hook in the first second · a change every 1.5–3 s · an open loop paid off at the peak · a re-hook at 40–60% · the last frame matches the first so it loops; no fade to black.
