# formats.md: proven reel formats, each with its mechanism

Used by 02_ideas (pick a format) and 03_script (follow its recipe). Every format names a studied
example in `examples/`. Pattern seen across the 10 studied reels: **the concept carries the reel,
not the editing.** 6 of 10 have 1–3 shots. The top two (1.9M and 258K likes) are the artist on
camera with a premise and an ask. Fast cutting appears only where a visual system motivates it.

| Format | Premise shape | Mechanism (why it works) | Footage it needs | Edit recipe | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Artist challenge** | "I'm a ___ and I have only N pieces to convince you to follow me" | direct address + a stake + an ask the viewer answers in comments ("did it work?") | the artist to camera, playful, 3–5 s; N best pieces, each framed the same way | talking shot with premise text → N pieces ~2 s each, best last → no end card; the caption asks | `examples/DfaKGRTMHqK.md` |
| **Then vs now** | "2021" / "2026" labels on screen | a growth arc in one take; escalation to an absurd scale; physical comedy | one locked wide take, the artist performing with old vs new work | single take, two static labels, music lift at the turn; the reveal is the last 4 s | `examples/DfwjNitOAwT.md` |
| **Studio skit (dialogue)** | a relatable client/artist exchange, deadpan | recognition humour; the punchline is the reaction | one wide static of the room, real speech, 2 people | dialogue captions line by line, one cut at most, the punchline caption lands last | `examples/DgGDKNDSc2-.md` |
| **Sign / prop gag** | one visual joke the viewer reads ("PLEASE DON'T TALK TO ME") | a 2-second read, then confirmation shots; studio culture | the prop in frame, 2–3 angles | 3 shots, ≤7 s, the caption extends the joke | `examples/Df0Kf7cRTro.md` |
| **Meaning / fandom quote** | a tattoo of a story people love + its iconic line | community recognition; the line's emotion transfers to the tattoo | the finished piece, 1–2 slow shots (the wash, the clean arm) | the voice line as audio, word-by-word captions in sync, 2 long shots | `examples/Df2nN7eoEG-.md` |
| **Effort contrast reveal** | "POV: the last detail of your 45-hour drawing" | the hours become awe; one continuous move from detail to whole | one macro shot of the last stroke that pulls back to the full work | one shot, POV text on top, the music swells into the full view | `examples/DgF4qoys11z.md` |
| **Cinematic process with an anchor** | a big piece from stencil to finish | a repeated locked wide frame shows growth; close details between; the finale moves to a new place | a filmer + a locked wide angle repeated per stage, details, a finale shot in a different setting | 1–2 beats per shot on the grid, the anchor wide every 3–5 shots, cuts on section changes, final held 5–7 s | `examples/DgGAQT5O6VT.md` |
| **Blank → art swap** | the same hand-held frame alternates blank and finished pieces | a rhythmic before/after; the loop rewatches | one hand, one frame, N blank sheets and N pieces | alternate blank/art on every beat, ~0.7 s each, ≤7 s, loops | `examples/DgFIGapO6U5.md` |
| **Product drop with a flip** | the original → making the copies → the stack | the making-of turns into an accelerating flipbook | signing, printing, a stack being flipped | 3 slow shots, then 0.1 s cuts through the stack on the beat | `examples/DcKkXqrs_vJ.md` |
| **Behind-the-camera POV** | "POV: you film tattoo artists" | shows the person behind the work; a premium look | the filmer at work + the cinematic results | 2–3 BTS shots with the POV text, then ~1 s cinematic shots on 2-beat cuts | `examples/Dfeh3XZMAA9.md` |

## Formats from the research, not yet studied as examples
- **"Things clients say" series**: exact phrases from real DMs, the artist's dry reaction after each; the 4th flips sweet.
- **Client meaning mini-doc**: open on the mirror reaction, then the client's own voice answering "what's this one for?", 3 b-roll shots, end on their hand touching the piece.
- **Expectation vs reality**: staged "expectation", real "reality" from a session (with consent).
- **Cover-up with stakes**: before, a "but", the sketch, the first look on the client's face.
- **Myth-buster**: the artist states the myth and the truth in one line; one proof shot. Saves and sends.

## Choosing
1. Start from the emotion and the sender (`story.md`), then pick the format whose mechanism delivers it.
2. Check the footage column against what exists or can be shot (`shooting.md`). No footage, no format.
3. Don't repeat the last post's format. Mix the series formats over a month.
