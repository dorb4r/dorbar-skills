# hooks.md: the first second

Used by 03_script. The hook is chosen *after* the story: it promises the peak the story delivers.

## Rules
- The viewer decides in 1–2 s; Instagram reports a skip rate for the first 3 s [S].
- **Frame 1 already moves or speaks.** Never a logo, a black frame, a slow wide shot or a still photo with text over it.
- **Speech or a person in the first 3 s**: speech lifted 10-second retention ~25% over music-only reels; a person on screen ~10% (Emplifi, 10,110 brand reels, 2026, Facebook data) [S].
- The hook must be true to what follows; misleading hooks are distributed less [S: Meta].
- It must read on mute: text or a face does the work, sound adds to it.

## Hook types that fit real footage
| Type | Frame 1 | Text / VO | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premise to camera | the artist looking at the lens, mid-gesture | the premise in one sentence | "I only have 4 drawings to convince you" (`examples/DfaKGRTMHqK.md`) |
| Foreshadowed peak | the client's laugh or tears, 0.5–1 s | none, or a question | open on the reaction, cut back to why |
| The odd detail | an extreme close-up that is hard to read | POV line that makes it make sense | "POV: the last detail of your 45-hour drawing" (`examples/DgF4qoys11z.md`) |
| A line of dialogue | two people, mid-conversation | the first spoken line as a caption | "Ok so are you ready?" (`examples/DgGDKNDSc2-.md`) |
| The sign | a prop the viewer reads in 1 s | the prop *is* the text | "PLEASE DON'T TALK TO ME" (`examples/Df0Kf7cRTro.md`) |
| Labels | the same frame split by two labels | "2021" / "2026" | `examples/DfwjNitOAwT.md` |
| A famous line | the work, with the line's voice | word-by-word captions | `examples/Df2nN7eoEG-.md` |

## Text hooks
- ≤ 7 words (≤ 6 in Hebrew), on screen from frame 1, inside the safe zone (`script.md`).
- First person, spoken language. Stakes, a number, a question or a POV. No marketing adjectives.
- Test: could another studio post this exact opening? If yes, it's generic: change it.

## Why "text over a slideshow" hooks fail
A statement over still photos ("everyone thinks I only do fine line…") gives no face, no motion, no
conflict and no reason to wait. The promise isn't visible, so the viewer can't predict a payoff.

## Hook checklist (on the script's first 3 s)
1. Something moves or speaks in frame 1.
2. A face or a voice within 3 s (unless the format is a pure reveal).
3. One promise, readable on mute in ≤ 1.5 s.
4. The peak it promises is actually in the reel.
5. Not interchangeable with another account's opening.
