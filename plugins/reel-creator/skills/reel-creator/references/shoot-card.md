# shoot-card.md: one page for the filmer (the artist never touches the phone)

Copy into the run as `04_shoot-card.md`, add the script's TO SHOOT rows at the top, print or send it.

**This reel needs (from the script):** _fill in: each TO SHOOT row, with its line or question_

**Before the client arrives (5 min)**
- [ ] Phone charged, 20 GB free, Do Not Disturb on, lens wiped
- [ ] 4K · 30 fps (60 for action) · HDR Video off · Auto FPS off · Lock White Balance on
- [ ] Lav charged, receiver on the phone, 10 s test clip played back with sound
- [ ] Studio music off or low. Mirror clean. Reveal spot decided. Light on the client's face
- [ ] 30 s of room tone (machine off, nobody talks)

**Check-in**
- [ ] Consent: "OK if I film a bit today? You'll see it first, you can stop me anytime." Then a yes on camera
- [ ] Lav on the client's chest, on the side away from the tattoo

**Shoot list**
1. [ ] Arrival wide: client enters or sits (hold 5 s)
2. [ ] Face before: nerves or smile, looking at the stencil (10 s, eye level)
3. [ ] THE WHY: "What's this one for?" → "Say it once more like I never heard it" → silent 2 s
4. [ ] Stencil in the mirror: their face
5. [ ] First line: needle on skin + their face (60 fps)
6. [ ] Process inserts ×3–5: hands, ink cap, wipe, top-down
7. [ ] Conversation: 30–60 s of them talking or laughing
8. [ ] Mid-session question: "Worse or easier than you thought?"
9. [ ] Transition bits: hand over the lens, one whip pan, a repeated wipe from 2 angles
10. [ ] Anchor wide (big pieces): the same locked frame at each stage
11. [ ] REVEAL: pre-framed on the mirror, 60 fps, roll 5 s before "ready?" to 15 s after. One take
12. [ ] After: hug or photo together, "one sentence: what do you see?"
13. [ ] Beauty shot of the piece (wiped, soft light), 2 angles
14. [ ] Exit: they walk out of frame

**During work**
- Every clip: 2 s before, 2 s after. Statics 10 s. Exposure locked on the skin.
- Vertical for faces; nothing important in the top or bottom fifth.
- Film in bursts, then put the phone down and be in the room.
- Never ask the artist to pause or pose. Never stage a reaction. Client wants quiet → stop filming them.

**Before they leave:** WHY · FACE BEFORE · REVEAL FACE on the phone? Missing one → ask the why now, by the mirror.
