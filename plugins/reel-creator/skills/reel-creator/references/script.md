# script.md: writing the beat sheet

Used by 03_script. The script is text first; pictures are chosen to serve it [S: Kallaway].

## Order of writing
1. **Premise**, one sentence: "When ___, ___ happens, BUT ___, SO ___."
2. **Target emotion** and **sender** (from the brief).
3. **Last line / last image**, then **hook** (`hooks.md`), then the middle.
4. **Beat table** (below). Then read the words alone: do they still land? Then the mute test: do picture + text alone land?
5. **Compress**: delete every beat the story survives without [S: Kallaway].

## Beat table (the script file's core)
| # | Time | Picture (real or to shoot) | Spoken / VO | On-screen text | Sound | Emotion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–1.5 | hook frame | first line | ≤ 6 words | hit / speech | curiosity |
| 2 | … | setup | "but…" | | | tension |
| 3 | … | turn / escalation | "so…" | | | |
| 4 | peak | the payoff on a face or the work | | | drop / silence → hit | laugh / awe / warmth |
| 5 | end | fast close that loops or lands a line | | | tail on the beat | send trigger |

Each row names its **source**: an existing file (path + in-point) or `TO SHOOT: <shot>`.
A row that says what a step proves (e.g. "we decide together") must show that step on screen.

## Length and pace
- 7–15 s for skits and gags, 15–30 s for stories and process. Past 30 s only if every beat earns it.
- Change something every 1.5–3 s (shot, size, speed, text or sound); a held shot is fine if something inside it changes [S].
- Re-hook at 40–60%: a new angle, a sound gap, "and then…" [O].
- Ending: on the peak's tail or a callback to frame 1 so it loops. A CTA card breaks the loop: keep it ≤ 2.5 s, or put the ask in the post caption ("Did it work?", "Anyone with a big wall?").

## Words on screen
- Spoken, first-person language; no marketing adjectives, no exclamation stacks.
- 2–7 words per card, ≤ 2 lines, ≤ ~25 characters per line. On screen ≥ max(1.2 s, 0.4 s × words). Hebrew: assume 12–15 characters/s [O].
- At most 5–6 cards in 30 s; picture tells, text annotates. ≥ 0.2 s gap between cards.
- Line breaks after punctuation; never split a noun from its adjective or a verb from its negation [S: Netflix].
- Kinetic captions: 1–3 words per chunk, **one** emphasis word in an accent colour [S]. Never every word bouncing.
- Speech captions only for words actually said, confirmed by a human ear.

## Safe zones (1080×1920)
Keep text and the key subject out of the top ~270 px, the bottom ~670 px and ~65 px each side (Meta: 14% / 35% / 6%) [S]. Keep the right ~130 px clear in the lower half (the action column). Centre-align RTL captions. Text never covers the work or a face.

## The script check (before any footage search or shoot)
Kill checklist (`story.md`) passes · the hook checklist (`hooks.md`) passes · every row has a source or a TO SHOOT · the words alone land · the mute test lands · the length matches the format.
